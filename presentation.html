SLIDE 02 / 15
PROBLEM STATEMENT

⚠️ Core Challenges in Campus Placement Preparation

  • Scattered & Unstructured Resources: Students waste 100+ hours hopping across YouTube, LeetCode, and Telegram PDFs without a daily plan.
  • Generic Test Series vs Real Drives: Traditional platforms test generic math instead of matching company-specific selection rounds (Amazon, TCS NQT, Cognizant GenC).
  • Lack of Instant Feedback: Students have no way to evaluate ATS resume match, STAR method HR answers, or code complexity without high costs.
  • Server Dependencies & Internet Outages: Most platforms break without active cloud connections, interrupting offline study sessions.
SLIDE 03 / 15
EXECUTIVE SOLUTION

💡 PlaceEdge — The Complete All-in-One Solution

🌐 100% Offline-First Architecture

Runs smoothly in browser storage (LocalStorage + IndexedDB mirror) with zero server delay or downtime.

🤖 AI-Native Assistance

Milo 🐾 AI Buddy provides sub-second technical coaching, code complexity analysis, ATS reviews, and HR feedback in 100% Pure English.

🗓️ Structured Daily Roadmaps

Includes a 30-Day Placement Sprint, 45-Min Timed Company Mock Tests, and Top 10 Company Selection Roadmaps.

📊 Real PDF Data Intelligence

Built from analyzing 75 company interview PDFs (2025 & 2026 batches) with hot-topic tags and company questions.
